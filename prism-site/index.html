FAQ

Quick answers

The questions we hear most. Still stuck? The form above goes straight to a human.

Are your phones really unlocked?

Yes — every phone we sell is factory unlocked and works with any carrier worldwide. No contracts, no SIM restrictions, and no hidden activation fees.

How does trade‑in work?

Add your current phone during checkout and answer four quick questions about its condition. We lock in your quote for 30 days, deduct it from your order instantly, and our courier collects the old device when delivering the new one.

What does Prism Care cover?

Two years of accidental damage protection — cracked screens, liquid damage, drops — with up to two claims per year and a flat ৳29 service fee. It's included free with every phone; theft and loss cover can be added for ৳149.

Can I return a phone if I change my mind?

Absolutely. You have 30 days from delivery for a full refund, no questions asked. We'll send a prepaid label, or the courier can collect it from your door.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Spread the cost over 12 or 24 months at 0% APR, or split any order into three interest-free payments. A soft credit check that doesn't affect your score is all it takes.
